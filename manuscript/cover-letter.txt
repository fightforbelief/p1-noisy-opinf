Dear Editors of Numerical Algorithms,

Please consider “A two-time-scale law for lagged operator inference on smooth trajectories” as a research article.

The submission concerns a numerical property of two-stage least squares on smooth trajectory samples. The classical angular bound describes worst-case sensitivity to a regression residual. We derive the leading signed change for a specified moving response as the lag spacing and forecast horizon shrink independently. For two lags, taking the limits sequentially misses three quarters of the leading displacement when the intervals are equal.

A Lorenz–96 experiment varied the two intervals independently and tested predictions fixed before the lagged fits. In the prespecified small-step region the joint formula had block-median relative errors of 2.36%–2.74%, even though the changes themselves were only 4.26–6.15 parts per million of the state increment. Separate measured-data studies tested whether a coefficient estimated in an earlier period transferred when the fitted difference was materially large. Six tide gauges qualified for one lag. Neither tide height nor vertical foot force yielded a repeated two-lag pass on the tested grids. We report this limit on practical use alongside the proof and the controlled tests of the mixed term.

The manuscript, source code, protocols, signed matrix forecasts and complete block-level scores are available at https://github.com/fightforbelief/p1-noisy-opinf.
