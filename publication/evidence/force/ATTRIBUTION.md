The time and two total-force columns in observations.npz are extracted from Jeffrey Hausdorff, *Gait in Parkinson's Disease*, version 1.0.0, PhysioNet (2008). The archive contains only the first trial of the twelve specified control participants. The original records include additional sensor columns.
